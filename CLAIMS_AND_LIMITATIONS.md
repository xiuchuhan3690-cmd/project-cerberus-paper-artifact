# Claims and limitations

Within the frozen finite Narrow Layered Core model and declared local synthetic evaluation, the integrated mechanisms preserved eight specified bounded safety properties without a reported contradiction. Public files here let readers inspect selected original code/models, copied non-sensitive result records, and a separate implementation of evidence consistency checks. The public quick suite is a new derivative check, not historical requalification.

The artifact does **not** establish universal security, production readiness, complete Active Directory recovery, all Windows AD semantics, enterprise-scale performance, real-world attack resistance, complete hidden-influence discovery, distributed-consensus correctness, independent semantic proof, external-system finality, or guaranteed progress. Closed schema, finite transform/influence inventories, trusted cryptography/quorum, abstract effect adapters, and single-host slot linearizability remain assumptions. TC-7 lacks a targeted final-matrix toggle. M8-T3 is a synthetic state-count projection, not complete mechanism throughput.

The verifier is implementation-separated from producers but shares declarative rules. Agreement cannot rule out a common specification error. Unknown critical influence and ambiguous external effects lead to exclusion/quarantine/HOLD, not an availability guarantee.
